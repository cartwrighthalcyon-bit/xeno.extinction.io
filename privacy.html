XENO.IO Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

XENO.IO is an online game made by Halcyon. This policy explains what information the game collects when you play on the website or the Android app, why it is collected, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Game account. When you first open the game it creates an account for you automatically. It stores a random account ID, the player name you choose, your game progress (aliens, currencies, story progress, settings) and your scores for the leaderboards.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and first name from Google so you can load your progress on other devices. We do not store your email address or your Google password.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number or Google Pay or Apple Pay details. We keep a record of what you bought so it stays on your account.
  • Chat and reports. Messages in online matches are filtered and passed between players in real time; we do not keep a history of chat. If you report a player, we store the report so we can review it.
  • Technical data. Our servers see your IP address when you connect, which is used briefly to prevent abuse (for example, limiting how many accounts one network can create) and appears in short-lived server logs.
  • On your device. The game saves your progress and a sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Advertising

The game may show ads between matches through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies or device identifiers to show and measure ads. You can learn more and control ad personalization at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

How we use it

Only to run the game: saving your progress, showing leaderboards, delivering purchases, running online matches, keeping chat safe, and preventing cheating and abuse. We do not sell your information.

Who we share it with

  • Render, which hosts our servers and database in the United States.
  • Stripe, which processes payments (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Google, for optional sign-in and for ads.
  • Other players see your player name, chosen alien and scores.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept until you delete your account. Purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

Deleting your account

Open the game, go to Settings, and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, progress, purchases and scores from our servers. You can also clear the game's data from your browser or uninstall the app to remove what is stored on your device.

Children

XENO.IO is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
